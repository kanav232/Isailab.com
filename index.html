Why now01 / 08

The future will be led by those who lead with AI.

AI is rewriting the rules of the world. It creates art and music, writes code, designs products, diagnoses disease, runs businesses, powers cities and, more and more, carries out entire tasks on its own.

Every field a student might step into is being transformed by it.

MedicineLawDesignFinanceScienceTechnologyEntrepreneurship

Knowing how to use AI will be common.

Knowing how to lead with it will be rare.

The leaders of tomorrow will direct AI towards what matters, make wise decisions with it, bring people and AI together, and do it all with integrity. That is AI leadership. It is the defining skill of the next generation, and it can be learned, starting now.

Introducing the program02 / 08

This is not

  • a summer class
  • a coding course
  • a lecture series.

A program for the generation that will lead the age of AI.

The Indo Youth AI Leadership Program is a six-day leadership experience at IIIT Delhi, one of India's leading institutes for computing. It brings together driven students from across India and gives them something few young people ever get: the chance to think, create, decide and lead with AI, guided by the people who are building the future.

What it is

A selective program with a rigorous selection process, a real university campus, and an experience shaped by an AI research lab working at the frontier of safe and trustworthy AI for India.

The experience03 / 08

Six days that feel like the future.

01

Learn where the future is built

For six days, the campus of IIIT Delhi becomes your classroom: its research labs, its innovation spaces and the energy of university life.

02

Learn from the people shaping AI

Masterclasses by faculty, AI researchers, founders and industry leaders, and candid conversations with young founders who started exactly where you are now.

03

Create with the most powerful tools of our time

In studios and labs, ideas become prototypes, with AI as your creative partner and expert mentors by your side. See AI at work in the real world beyond campus, and learn to lead it with integrity from a lab dedicated to making AI safe and trustworthy.

04

Take the stage

On the final day, every participant steps onto the stage of the Pitch Summit to present their ideas before a jury of experts and the chief guest.

05

Stand among the best

Every participant earns their place through an application and a Group Discussion round, and spends six days among peers as ambitious and curious as they are.

The campus

At IIIT Delhi.

Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology, Delhi is a research-led state university, established in 2008 by an Act of the Government of Delhi, and widely regarded as one of India's leading institutes for computing and its allied fields. Its campus in Okhla brings together departments spanning computer science, electronics, design, the social sciences and more. Every regular faculty member holds a doctorate, many from leading universities around the world.

For six days, participants learn on this campus and step inside its research labs and innovation spaces.

ISAiL (Indo Secure AI Lab) is a multidisciplinary research and engineering initiative dedicated to advancing AI that is safe and trustworthy for India.

AI must be built safely, governed well and deployed responsibly.

The lab brings together technologists, policy thinkers and domain specialists to research, build and deploy AI across three pillars.

Pillar 01

Technical alignment and security.

Model safety evaluations, red-teaming, prompt security and safety alignment, including for Indic and regional languages.

Pillar 02

Sovereign strategy and e-governance.

Technology policy, sovereign compute, data privacy, and how public institutions can use AI to serve citizens better.

Pillar 03

Real-world deployment.

Scalable AI for public good in areas such as healthcare, agriculture and education, across 22+ regional languages.

Techno-civic stewardship.

Treating AI not only as a technology, but as a responsibility. The Indo Youth AI Leadership Program brings that same spirit to the next generation of leaders.

QuestionsFAQ

Frequently asked.

Something else on your mind? Write to contact@isailab.com.

Do I need to know AI, coding or business?

No. Everything starts from the basics. The program is about leading with AI, not building it.

Is it only for students who want a career in technology?

No. AI is shaping every field, so the program is designed for students of every stream. Teams deliberately mix grades and streams.

Is accommodation available?

Limited on-campus residence is available for separate charges, subject to availability. You can request it when you apply.

What should I bring?

Warm clothing, personal essentials, a notebook, and the documents listed in your confirmation email.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Every participant receives a certificate of participation.

How are participants selected?

Through a three-step process: an online application, a Group Discussion round for shortlisted applicants, and final selection. Selected students receive a confirmation email and secure their seat by completing the payment.
