Who we are

Building the people and infrastructure who will shape how India builds AI.

"The question is no longer whether AI will define our future, but who will build it safely, govern it wisely, and deploy it responsibly."

Indo Secure AI Labs (ISAiL) exists to answer that question. We're a multidisciplinary research and engineering initiative advancing safe, trustworthy AI for India — spanning technical research, model development, applied engineering, and real-world deployment.

We bring together technologists, policy thinkers, and domain specialists to build solutions that matter and run bootcamps and fellowships that bring bright, curious minds from every academic stream into that work.

Instead of treating AI purely as a coding tool, ISAiL operates at the intersection of technical safety, public policy, and real-world impact, building techno-civic stewardship for students, founders, and young leaders across Science, Commerce, and Humanities.

The three pillars

One lab, three disciplines.

ISAiL runs original research, ships models and projects, and deploys solutions where they're needed most, organized around three foundational pillars.

Indo Secure AI Labs
01 / Core Tech

Technical Alignment & Security

Model safety evaluations, adversarial red-teaming, prompt security, and safety alignment, with special emphasis on Indic models and regional language safety.

02 / Policy

Sovereign Strategy & e-Governance

Macro tech policy, sovereign compute infrastructure, and data privacy law, exploring how public institutions can use AI for governance and civic service delivery safely.

03 / Impact

Real-World Deployment

Practical, scalable AI for public good: rural healthcare diagnostics, agricultural forecasting, education, and bridging the digital divide across 22+ regional languages.

How to join

Eligibility

Seats are limited. Early application is strongly encouraged. Review of applications may begin before the stated deadline.

Eligibility criteria

  • Currently enrolled in Grade 9, 10, 11, or 12 at a recognized school, in any academic stream (Science, Commerce, or Humanities).
  • Have completed Grade 12 (or equivalent) in 2026, and are awaiting or have received results.
  • No prior coding experience, business background, or technical qualification required. Candidates are evaluated on curiosity, aptitude, and a one-page Statement of Purpose.
