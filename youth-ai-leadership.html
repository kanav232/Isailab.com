Quick answers.

Do I need to know AI, coding or business?

No. Everything starts from the basics. It's about leading with AI, not building it.

I'm not into tech. Is this for me?

Yes. AI is shaping every field, so the program is built for every stream. Teams mix grades and streams on purpose.

What does the fee include?

The program fee of ₹13,000 covers the program, meals, the program kit and access to campus facilities such as the gym, library and labs. On-campus residence is an additional ₹9,000.

Is financial aid available?

Yes. Need-based scholarships of up to 75% of the program fee are available, subject to verification of relevant documents and review by a committee, whose decision on the final offer is final. Choose "Yes" for the scholarship question in the form and apply by 20 November 2026.

How does selection work?

In four steps. You fill in the online application. We review every application and invite shortlisted candidates to a Group Discussion round (these begin on 1 November 2026). Selected students then receive a confirmation email and secure their seat by completing the payment. Final selection runs from 1 to 20 December 2026.

Can I stay on campus?

Limited on-campus residence is available for an additional ₹9,000, subject to availability. Request it in your application.

I finished Grade 12 this year. Can I still apply?

Yes. Students who completed Grade 12 in 2026 can apply, along with students currently in Grades 9 to 12.

What does a day look like?

Program hours are 9 AM to 8 PM. Days mix masterclasses, hands-on studios and labs, team work and evenings like Chai with Changemakers and a Cultural Evening. Residents also have morning routines and evening walks and discussions with their mentors and teams.

Who will I learn from?

Faculty, AI and technology practitioners, entrepreneurs, young founders, designers, finance and marketing professionals, and career experts. Mentors guide you throughout the six days.

What should I bring?

Warm clothing (it's Delhi in December), personal essentials, a notebook, and the documents listed in your confirmation email.

What language is the program in?

English. You should be comfortable learning and communicating in English.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every participant receives a certificate of participation.
