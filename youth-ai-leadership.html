Quick answers.

Do I need to know AI, coding or business?

No. Everything starts from the basics. It's about leading with AI, not building it.

I'm not into tech. Is this for me?

Yes. AI is shaping every field, so the program is built for every stream. Teams mix grades and streams on purpose.

Can I stay on campus?

Limited on-campus residence is available for separate charges, subject to availability. Request it in your application.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every participant receives a certificate of participation.
